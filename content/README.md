# Content

Everything the site shows lives here as JSON. `pnpm validate:content` checks shape and cross references.

Status of the Bangkok set (2026-10-15):

- Areas and sub-areas: real names, approximate centres.
- Stations: extracted from OpenStreetMap (ODbL), coordinates are real.
- Landmark pins: real places with coordinates written from general knowledge, accurate to roughly 100 m. Verify each against OpenStreetMap before launch. 29 more added 2026-10-15 for the weekend plan (3 to 5 per area, each with `when`); their coordinates are being checked against Nominatim on the Mac.
- Hotel pins: 24 real hotels, three per area (luxury, mid, budget), a starting set to work with, not editorial picks. Each one is a hotel or hostel object in OpenStreetMap (coordinates from Nominatim, 2026-10-15). The OSM ids are in the commit that added them. The `why` line only states the location. Links (2026-10-15): exact hotel pages, plain URLs without affiliate parameters. Booking.com `hotel/th/<slug>.html` for 22 hotels, each page title checked against the hotel name; the other 2 (The Quarter Ari, Hotel Royal Bangkok) keep a Booking.com name search because Booking's search did not return them. Agoda `<slug>/hotel/bangkok-th.html` for 23 hotels, found through Agoda's own suggestion search and matched by Agoda hotel id (Agoda pages render their title in the browser, so the id is the check). Three first picks with no exact page on either site (The Iconic Hotel Ari, HQ Hostel, Oneday Hostel) were replaced on 2026-10-15 by Best Western Chatuchak (OSM N11680171970), Kinnon Deluxe Hostel (N11012982038) and H-District Boutique Poshotel (W1298094980); for each, Booking's own map position matches the OSM object. Booking blocks plain curl with a 202 bot check, so check links in a browser.
- Hotel prices (`price`): per night in THB. Every hotel currently carries `basis: "tier-estimate"`, a single typical figure for its class (budget 600, mid 1,800, luxury 5,500) so the price slider has something to work on; the site labels these "typical for its class, not checked". Replace with `basis: "checked"` plus `checkedOn` once a real rate has been read from the hotel's booking page.
- Hotel walk minutes: straight line at 75 m per minute to the nearest station *listed on the area*, rounded up. Where the nearest real station is not on the area's list (Phrom Phong, Saphan Khwai, Surasak, Khao San's walk to the river piers), the number is longer than the real walk.
- Walk minutes: estimates, not measured routes.
- A pin's `when` (times of day it is open or at its best, days if not every day, an optional note) drives the "When" filter and the line on the card. Editorial: written from general knowledge of opening hours in the 2026-10 content pass, not checked against each venue; a wrong one is a content bug to fix in the area file.
- Place shapes (`shapes/<area>/<pin>.geojson`): a street's real road stretch (MultiLineString) or a zone's real area such as a market (MultiPolygon), from OpenStreetMap (ODbL) via `scripts/fetch-place-shapes.mjs`, referenced from the pin's `shape`. Never drawn by hand. The explorer draws a street as a glowing line and a zone as a soft wash with its 3D buildings tinted.
- Area and sub-area outlines (`boundaries/<area>.geojson`, `boundaries/<area>/<sub>.geojson`): official Bangkok sub-districts (khwaeng) from OpenStreetMap (ODbL), merged by `scripts/area-boundaries.mjs` from `sources/bangkok-khwaeng.geojson` (fetched by `scripts/fetch-khwaeng.mjs`). Never drawn by hand. The mapping and every known mismatch are in [docs/boundaries.md](../docs/boundaries.md).
